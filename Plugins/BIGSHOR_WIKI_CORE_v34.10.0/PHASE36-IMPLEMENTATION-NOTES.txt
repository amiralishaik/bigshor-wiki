BIGSHOR WIKI CORE 29.1.0 — PHASE 36 EDITORIAL GOVERNANCE

BASELINE
- Existing Core 29.0.0 source archive.
- Parent/Child themes are not modified.
- Intended active theme compatibility baseline supplied by site owner: 21.0.1 / 21.0.1.

IMPLEMENTED
1. Separate public BIGSHOR News content type at /news/ with News Categories taxonomy.
2. Existing [bigshor_wiki_submit] shortcode upgraded to collect Knowledge or News submissions.
3. Guest submissions remain disabled; contributor submissions require login, wiki_contributor role and verified email (or editorial capability).
4. Required policy acceptance per submission, conflict disclosure, source URLs, and notability rationale for Knowledge.
5. Basic duplicate-title screening and ten-minute per-user submission cooldown.
6. Editorial review panel for Knowledge and News.
7. Required source adequacy and editorial checklist.
8. Knowledge publication requires a positive notability assessment.
9. First independent reviewer required for all submissions.
10. High-risk submissions require a second distinct reviewer; author cannot review their own submission.
11. Publication guard blocks direct publication until applicable evidence checks and approvals are saved.
12. Editorial governance dashboard and local audit event trail.
13. [bigshor_news_recent] shortcode for published News articles.

IMPORTANT OPERATING NOTES
- Reviewer decisions must be saved before clicking Publish. If the first Publish attempt is blocked, save the completed review record and publish again.
- High-risk status is editor-controlled; editors must classify sensitive content conservatively.
- Existing published articles are not automatically re-reviewed or unpublished.
- Existing guest submission route remains unavailable; the shortcode requires authentication.
- No paid editorial submission or payment workflow is added.
- Theme files are deliberately unchanged.

- Test direct REST/API publication attempts and any other plugins/integrations that can publish wiki_article or bsw_news.
